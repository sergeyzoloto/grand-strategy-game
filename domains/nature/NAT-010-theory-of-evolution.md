A mechanism (natural selection acting on heritable variation) that explains
the diversity and adaptation of living species without requiring a designer
— directly underlying this record's own immunological-capital and disease-
resistance mechanics (HLT's territory) as much as it reshapes biology as a
discipline.

## Timing

Darwin and Wallace jointly present the theory in 1858; Darwin's On the
Origin of Species (1859) elaborates the full argument and evidence; the
theory's implications and extensions (including for human evolution
specifically) are actively debated and extended through the rest of the
1800s.

## Mechanism

Natural selection requires only three conditions to operate — heritable
variation among individuals, differential survival and reproduction based
on that variation, and enough generations for the effect to accumulate —
producing adaptation and new species over time without requiring any
guiding intention, a genuinely novel explanatory mechanism distinct from
either divine design or the earlier, less rigorously mechanistic
transformist ideas some prior naturalists had gestured toward. The
mechanism only becomes plausible given a timescale of millions of years
for small effects to accumulate, which is what geology's deep time
(NAT-017) supplies and which Darwin credited directly, carrying Lyell's
Principles of Geology throughout the Beagle voyage.

## Social consequence

The theory directly displaces humanity's assumed special, designed status
in the natural order, provoking sustained religious and cultural resistance
in multiple societies that persists into the present far more durably than
resistance to most other scientific theories in this record — a reception
pattern comparable in kind, if not degree, to heliocentrism's (NAT-006)
earlier displacement of Earth's cosmic centrality.

## Regional specifics

This is a genuinely Europe-concentrated theoretical achievement in its
formal development. Al-Jahiz's ninth-century observations of environmental
pressure and struggle among species represent a real conceptual
precedent for pieces of the eventual theory — struggle for survival,
environmental influence on traits — without proposing the heritable-
variation mechanism or common descent that make Darwin and Wallace's
formulation a complete, testable theory rather than a set of suggestive
observations.

## Contested

Evolution by natural selection is not scientifically contested within
biology, but its social and religious reception remains genuinely and
persistently contested in specific societies, producing real ongoing
conflict in education and public policy. Model scientific validity and
social acceptance as fully independent variables — a theory can carry
essentially unanimous specialist consensus while remaining an active
domestic political battleground, with that acceptance gap itself worth
modeling as a social phenomenon rather than treated as evidence of
unresolved scientific dispute.

## Hook

Natural selection's heritable-variation mechanism directly underlies this
record's own `immunological capital` and disease-resistance mechanics —
and the theory's scientific completeness and its social acceptance are
independent variables, with the gap between near-universal specialist
consensus and persistent contested public reception (comparable to
heliocentrism's, NAT-006) itself worth modeling explicitly. The mechanism
depends directly on geology's deep time (NAT-017) for a plausible
timescale, and on systematic classification (NAT-021) for the comparable,
cumulative record of species similarity and variation the theory then
explains causally.
