This entry owns metaphysics specifically as a precursor framework shaping
what questions a society treats as answerable and by what method — the
systematic philosophical argument itself, independent of its role in
setting up later empirical inquiry, is PHI's fuller territory.

## Timing

Pre-Socratic natural philosophy in the Mediterranean and Nyaya-Vaisheshika
categorization in South Asia both develop from roughly -600; Chinese
correlative cosmology matures from around -400; Islamic falsafa's synthesis
project runs through the medieval period to around 1200.

## Mechanism

A metaphysical framework does real, load-bearing work for whatever
knowledge-generating activity follows it: it sets which questions are
considered meaningful to ask (what counts as a "cause," whether the world
is built from fixed unchanging substances or from relational, cyclical
processes), which in turn shapes what kind of answer later empirical or
mathematical inquiry (NAT-010, NAT-090) will even look for — a framework
that assumes fixed underlying substances motivates a different research
program than one built around cyclical relational categories.

## Social consequence

Whichever metaphysical framework a scholarly tradition inherits becomes the
largely invisible background assumption its later natural inquiry operates
within, rarely examined directly by working practitioners the way an
explicit theory would be — a scholar trained in one framework often finds
the other genuinely difficult to think inside, not merely unfamiliar.

## Regional specifics

Nyaya-Vaisheshika's categorization of reality and accompanying formal
epistemology reach a rigor comparable to Aristotelian metaphysics through
entirely independent argument, unsettling any assumption that systematic
metaphysical system-building is a uniquely Greek achievement. Chinese
correlative cosmology's relational, cyclical categories represent a
structurally different kind of explanatory project from the Greek and
Indian search for fixed underlying substances, not an earlier stage of the
same one. Islamic falsafa's explicit synthesis of inherited Greek
metaphysics with revealed theology produces a genuinely new combined
framework neither source tradition would have generated alone.

## Hook

The specific metaphysical framework a tradition inherits sets which
questions later empirical and mathematical inquiry (NAT-010, NAT-090) treats
as meaningful to ask in the first place — an invisible precondition for
knowledge-generation rather than a competing method, with real
consequences for which discoveries a tradition's method can even reach.
