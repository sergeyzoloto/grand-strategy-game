A general mathematical theory of motion and force — why things move, speed
up, slow down, and stay still — that eventually lets engineering (ENG-009's
steam engine and beyond) be designed from calculated principle rather than
purely from empirical trial and refinement.

## Timing

Aristotelian mechanics (from around -350) dominates for over a millennium
despite significant internal problems explaining projectile motion;
impetus theory develops in the Islamic world and transmits to Latin Europe
from around 1000-1300; Newton's Principia (1687) provides the mathematical
synthesis — laws of motion and universal gravitation — that finally
resolves the projectile-motion problem impetus theory had only partially
addressed.

## Mechanism

Aristotelian mechanics struggles specifically to explain why a thrown
object keeps moving after leaving the thrower's hand, since its framework
assumes continuous motion requires a continuously-applied mover — impetus
theory patches this by proposing the mover imparts an internal quality
that persists and gradually dissipates, a genuine conceptual advance toward
(though not identical to) Newtonian inertia, which finally resolves the
problem by proposing that motion, not rest, is the state requiring no
continued explanation at all.

## Social consequence

A validated general theory of mechanics lets engineering practice shift
from empirically refining what has worked before toward calculating what
should work before building it — a change in method that compounds over
time, since calculated designs can be extended to new, untested
configurations empirical refinement alone would need to approach far more
cautiously and slowly.

## Regional specifics

Islamic impetus theory's direct transmission into Latin Europe, where it
demonstrably shapes Buridan's and other late-medieval scholars' work ahead
of Newton's eventual synthesis, makes the Islamic contribution a genuine
intermediate step in the mechanics lineage rather than a separate parallel
tradition. Chinese mechanical engineering achievement — sophisticated
clockwork and astronomical instrumentation among other examples — proceeds
through empirical craft refinement without an accompanying general
mathematical theory of motion, showing practical mechanical sophistication
and theoretical mechanics are separable achievements.

## Hook

A validated general mechanical theory converts engineering from empirical
refinement into calculation from first principle, directly enabling later
engineering entries like the steam engine (ENG-009) to be designed rather
than merely discovered — with the Newtonian synthesis itself building on
a real, transmitted intermediate step (Islamic impetus theory) rather than
resolving the projectile-motion problem from a standing start.
