A dedicated, continuously-active civil institution for maintaining
internal order and enforcing law day to day — distinct from the military
(WAR-001), which is organized for external or large-scale internal
conflict rather than routine street-level order maintenance.

## Timing

Chinese baojia household-registration policing dates from around 1000;
Robert Peel's Metropolitan Police, widely credited as the first modern
professional civilian police force organized specifically for continuous
patrol and crime prevention, is established in 1829.

## Mechanism

Baojia achieves distributed order-maintenance by making small household
groups mutually responsible for each other's conduct — a structural
incentive for internal reporting and self-policing that requires minimal
dedicated state manpower, since the enforcement labor is distributed
across the population itself. The Metropolitan Police model instead
concentrates order-maintenance in a dedicated, salaried, professionally
trained civilian force — a fundamentally different mechanism (specialized
institution versus distributed mutual responsibility) achieving broadly
comparable order-maintenance function.

## Social consequence

Baojia's mutual-responsibility structure creates strong social pressure
for households to monitor and report on each other, since a group's
collective liability for a member's misconduct gives every household
member a direct stake in everyone else's compliance — a fundamentally
different social relationship to law enforcement than a professional
police force's more impersonal, specialized relationship to the population
it patrols.

## Regional specifics

Baojia's mutual-responsibility registration system, already covered for
its migration-control function, does double duty as this entry's
distributed policing mechanism — the same underlying administrative
`legibility` infrastructure serving two related but distinct order-
maintenance functions at once. Pre-1829 Europe's genuine institutional gap
— relying on a patchwork of watchmen and military intervention rather
than dedicated professional policing — shows the professional-police
model was a real innovation closing a specific gap, not simply a
refinement of an already-functioning comparable European institution.

## Hook

Internal order maintenance can run through a dedicated, specialized
professional institution (Peel's model) or through distributed mutual
household responsibility (baojia) built on the same administrative
`legibility` infrastructure MIG-018 already uses for population control —
two structurally different mechanisms for the same underlying function,
neither requiring the other as a precondition.
