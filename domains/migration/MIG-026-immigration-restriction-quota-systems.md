The mirror image of MIG-015: where scheduled passenger service converts
mass migration into an ordinary household economic decision, a
legislated cap converts that same open channel into a closed or sharply
throttled one by unilateral destination-state act, independent of
anything happening on the sending side.

## Timing

The US Chinese Exclusion Act (1882) is the first major case of an
outright ethnicity-based immigration ban; Australia's Immigration
Restriction Act (1901) achieves comparable exclusion through an indirect
dictation-test mechanism; the US National Origins Act (1924) generalizes
the logic into a comprehensive quota system capping annual immigration by
country of origin, calculated specifically to favor prior-established
Northern European-origin populations over Southern/Eastern European and
Asian immigration.

## Mechanism

A destination state legislates a hard numerical or categorical cap on
immigration — by explicit ethnic exclusion, an indirect mechanism like a
discretionary language test, or a national-origin quota formula —
independent of any individual migrant's ability to pay passage, find
work, or otherwise qualify under the destination's own ordinary
economic-migration criteria. This converts a previously open or
lightly-regulated mass-migration channel (MIG-015) into a closed or
sharply throttled one by a single legislative act, rather than through
the gradual market saturation or destination-side wage decline that
would otherwise slow migration on its own.

## Social consequence

Restriction legislation falls unevenly by design — the same legal
channel that stays open for one national-origin group closes overnight
for another, converting a previously unified "immigrant" category into a
legally stratified one, and stranding migration-dependent communities
(return-visit plans, family reunification expectations, ongoing chain
migration) that had organized around an assumption of continued open
passage.

## Regional specifics

Australia's dictation-test mechanism achieves the same substantive
exclusion as America's explicit ethnic bans and quota formula through a
legally indirect, discretionarily-applied route — a different legal
technique producing an equivalent restriction outcome, showing the
underlying mechanic (a destination state closing a channel by unilateral
act) doesn't require identical legal form to achieve identical practical
effect. Europe's general absence of comparable restriction in this era
reflects its position as a net emigration source rather than a
destination under mass-migration pressure — the mechanic is specifically
a response available to, and adopted by, destination states.

## Hook

An event distinct from MIG-017's general documentation-gate mechanic: a
sudden, legislated closure of a specific existing channel, applied
unevenly by national-origin or ethnicity rather than as a general
administrative-permission layer applied to everyone alike — the
destination-side mirror to MIG-015's channel-opening.
