Zoroastrianism argues the cosmos is the real battleground between two
independent, opposed principles, with every individual's free moral choice
actually mattering to which side accumulates strength toward an eventual,
linear, one-time cosmic resolution — a structurally distinct metaphysical
starting point from every other cosmology-of-ethics entry in this domain.

## Timing

The Gathas, the oldest core hymns traditionally attributed to Zoroaster
himself, carry a genuinely disputed compositional date ranging from roughly
-1700 to -600 depending on linguistic-dating method; this entry's era window
(-1200 to -500) reflects the broad range most commonly argued for rather
than a single settled point, with the tradition's doctrinal elaboration
continuing well past either end of that range.

## Mechanism

Zoroastrian cosmology posits two independent, opposed principles — Ahura
Mazda (truth, order, light) and Angra Mainyu (the lie, chaos, darkness) — locked
in real cosmic conflict, with human beings possessing genuine free moral
choice whose exercise actually adds strength to one side or the other rather
than merely reflecting a pre-determined cosmic balance: every individual's
choice between truth (asha) and the lie (druj) is cosmically consequential in
itself, converting personal ethical conduct into direct participation in an
ongoing cosmic contest rather than a matter of self-cultivation, role-
fulfillment, or personal liberation from illusion.

## Social consequence

Because moral choice is framed as directly consequential to a real cosmic
outcome rather than as primarily a matter of individual self-cultivation or
liberation, Zoroastrian ethics places unusually strong weight on active,
visible good conduct — good thoughts, good words, good deeds — as a
continuous contribution to the cosmic contest's eventual resolution, rather
than as a private virtue whose social payoff is chiefly the practitioner's
own improved character or eventual liberation.

## Regional specifics

Zoroastrianism's linear, one-time eschatological resolution (a final cosmic
victory of truth over the lie, followed by a renovated world) is a
structurally different time-model from Vedanta's liberation-through-
identity-recognition (PHI-040) and Buddhism's cyclical rebirth ended by
extinguishing craving (PHI-090) — both of those frameworks treat individual
liberation as escape from an ongoing cycle, where Zoroastrian ethics treats
individual conduct as cumulative contribution toward a single collective
cosmic ending every participant's choices actually help determine.

## Contested

The Gathas' compositional dating ranges from roughly -1700 to -600 depending
on linguistic-dating method, one of the most unsettled chronological
disputes in the history-of-religion literature. Model date_precision as
era-level and treat any more specific claimed date, including this entry's
own working window, as itself a contested position rather than a resolved
fact.

## Hook

A modifier/branch: linear-eschatological moral dualism as a distinct
cosmology-of-ethics option, standing alongside PHI-040's monism and
PHI-090's cyclical framework as three genuinely different, non-reducible
metaphysical starting points for grounding what makes ethical choice matter
in the first place.
