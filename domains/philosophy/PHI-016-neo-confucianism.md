Song-dynasty scholars, Zhu Xi foremost among them, respond to centuries of
Buddhist and Daoist metaphysical sophistication (PHI-012, PHI-007) by
building classical Confucian ethics (PHI-004) a full cosmological
apparatus of its own — converting an ethics of correctly performed social
roles into a complete philosophical system able to compete on Buddhism's
and Daoism's own metaphysical terrain.

## Timing

Neo-Confucian synthesis develops across the Song dynasty from roughly
1000, with Zhu Xi (1130–1200) producing its most influential and
lastingly authoritative systematization by the century's end.

## Mechanism

Zhu Xi's synthesis organizes reality around li (principle — the ideal,
rational pattern underlying each thing) and qi (vital-material force —
the actual physical stuff any particular thing is made of), with human
moral cultivation reframed as bringing one's own qi into clearer
alignment with one's inherently good li, obscured by turbid or unbalanced
qi — a metaphysical vocabulary sophisticated enough to directly engage
Buddhist arguments about the nature of mind and reality on comparable
terms, while officially condemning Buddhism and Daoism as heterodox.

## Social consequence

Because Neo-Confucianism explicitly frames itself as recovering the
"true" original meaning of the classical Confucian texts against later
corruption and foreign (Buddhist) influence, it can absorb genuinely
significant conceptual material from the traditions it condemns while
still claiming unbroken orthodox continuity — a durable synthesis whose
scale of Buddhist and Daoist conceptual borrowing, versus its own claimed
pure classical continuity, is a real point some scholars of Chinese
intellectual history push back on.

## Regional specifics

Neo-Confucianism's systematized curriculum — most consequentially Zhu
Xi's edited and annotated "Four Books" — becomes the fixed official
curriculum for China's imperial examination system (LAW-004) from the
Yuan dynasty's 1313 adoption onward, meaning this specific 12th-century
philosophical synthesis directly reshapes the content of a state
institution founded seven centuries earlier, rather than merely
influencing elite intellectual culture at one remove from state power.

## Hook

Neo-Confucianism gives classical ritual-and-role ethics (PHI-004) a
metaphysical apparatus capable of matching Buddhist and Daoist
sophistication on their own terrain, and the resulting synthesis doesn't
stay confined to philosophical debate — it becomes fixed, centuries
later, as the literal examination curriculum (LAW-004) an entire empire's
administrative recruitment pipeline runs on.
