A fixed period of enforced isolation for people or goods arriving from a
suspect location — the first health intervention that requires real state
coercive capacity rather than household or community practice.

## Timing

The term and the specific 40-day port-isolation model originate in Ragusa
(Dubrovnik) and Venice from 1377, in direct response to Black Death exposure;
cordon-sanitaire land-border variants follow over the succeeding centuries
wherever plague recurs.

## Mechanism

Quarantine works by trading economic throughput (delayed goods, idled ships
and crews, restricted movement) for a reduction in the probability an
incoming case seeds a new local outbreak — a bet that only pays off
statistically and is invisible when it succeeds (no outbreak occurs, which
looks identical to no outbreak having been likely anyway). It requires a state
capable of actually enforcing isolation at a chokepoint, which is why it first
appears in city-states with concentrated port control rather than in more
diffuse polities.

## Social consequence

Quarantine imposes its real cost asymmetrically on merchants, sailors, and
travelers — exactly the people whose livelihood depends on movement — while
the stationary population bears little of the direct cost and most of the
benefit, a tension that recurs every time quarantine measures are proposed
or resisted throughout this history.

## Regional specifics

Mediterranean port quarantine and Islamic plague jurisprudence reach broadly
convergent practical behavior (restrict movement from stricken areas) from
different starting justifications — one administrative-commercial, one
religious-legal — and the Islamic case additionally carries an internal
tension between movement restriction and theological objections to fleeing
what many contemporary scholars read as divinely sent. Qing China's baojia-
based household registration achieves comparable population-level movement
control through bureaucratic legibility already built for taxation and
policing, rather than needing a dedicated quarantine institution — the same
functional outcome from repurposing a different existing capability.

## Hook

Quarantine capability requires a `legibility`/chokepoint-control threshold to
unlock at all, and once available functions as a player-triggerable trade of
short-term economic throughput for reduced outbreak-seeding probability on
incoming exposure events.
