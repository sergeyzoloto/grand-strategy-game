Gunpowder's chemistry and the firearm as artifact are ENG's; this entry owns
what happens to infantry doctrine and army financing once a state actually
fields gunpowder-armed troops at scale — displacing armored shock cavalry
and complicating the fortification doctrine of WAR-005.

## Timing

Chinese gunpowder weapons see sustained military use from the 900s onward;
the technology and associated infantry doctrine diffuse westward, reaching
mature battlefield-decisive integration in the Middle East and Europe by the
1400s-1500s.

## Mechanism

A gunpowder firearm requires far less lifetime training investment to reach
lethal proficiency than a longbow or a lifetime of mounted swordsmanship,
which lets a state re-arm a much larger share of its infantry to a
militarily effective standard than earlier missile or shock weapons allowed
— but firearms and the powder, shot, and (once standardized) uniform
equipment they require also demand continuous state purchasing and supply in
a way a self-armed levy never did, tying effective gunpowder infantry
directly to the same standing-army fiscal capacity WAR-001 describes, now at
a higher and more continuous cost.

## Social consequence

Because firearms proficiency is comparatively fast to train relative to
elite cavalry or archery traditions, gunpowder infantry erodes the social
and battlefield primacy of aristocratic shock cavalry (WAR-006) built on a
lifetime of costly equestrian training — a lower-status, mass-trainable
soldier can now reliably defeat a higher-status, individually superior one,
directly threatening the social hierarchy the older cavalry-dominant system
had rested on.

## Regional specifics

China's centuries-long head start in both the chemistry and the military
doctrine of gunpowder weapons makes it the clear origin point for this
entire technology, a fact regularly underweighted in accounts that treat
gunpowder warfare as a European story starting with 15th-century cannon.
Ottoman integration of firearms into an already-professionalized standing
army structure produces a notably smoother institutional transition than
several European cases, where gunpowder infantry had to displace an
entrenched, politically powerful aristocratic cavalry class actively
resistant to its own obsolescence. Mughal India's more state-arsenal-
concentrated adoption pattern shows gunpowder integration doesn't have to
follow the broad-infantry-diffusion model to be militarily decisive.

## Hook

Gunpowder infantry converts battlefield effectiveness from a function of
lifetime individual training investment into a function of continuous state
purchasing capacity — directly eroding aristocratic shock-cavalry primacy
(WAR-006) and tying army financing (touching FIN) to sustained supply
procurement rather than one-time equipping.
