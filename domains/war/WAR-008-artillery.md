Gun-founding technique is ENG's; this entry owns artillery as an organized
military arm — how guns are massed, positioned, and coordinated with
infantry and siege operations (WAR-005), and what maintaining a siege train
costs a state to sustain.

## Timing

Purpose-built siege artillery becomes strategically decisive by the mid-
1400s (the fall of Constantinople, 1453, being the paradigm case); mobile
field-artillery doctrine, integrating guns directly into open-battle
tactics rather than reserving them for sieges, matures more gradually
through the following two centuries.

## Mechanism

An artillery train is an enormously expensive, logistically demanding asset
— casting, transporting, and supplying large guns requires a scale of state
fiscal and logistical capacity beyond what equipping infantry (WAR-007)
alone demands — but it fundamentally rewrites siege-attrition calculus
(WAR-005) by letting a besieger breach walls that centuries of fortification
design had made effectively impregnable to earlier methods, converting a
siege from a patience contest into an engineering-and-firepower one.

## Social consequence

Because only a state with substantial centralized fiscal capacity can
sustain an effective siege train, artillery ownership becomes a marker of
first-rank state power in a way individual infantry firearms (WAR-007)
never quite were — a state's artillery park size is legible, comparable,
and directly correlated with its capacity to reduce any fortification a
rival can build, reshaping the balance of power between centralized states
and the smaller fortified polities artillery makes newly vulnerable.

## Regional specifics

The Ottoman siege of Constantinople treats artillery as a dedicated,
purpose-organized strategic corps rather than an ad hoc addition, among the
earliest and clearest cases of gunpowder artillery being wielded as a
decisive strategic asset in its own right. South Asian armies' slower
integration of artillery into mobile field-battle tactics, keeping it more
centralized as a siege and prestige asset, shows adopting the gun-founding
technology (ENG's territory) and adopting the full doctrinal integration of
that technology into open-battle tactics are separable steps that different
states can take at very different speeds.

## Hook

An artillery train converts concentrated state fiscal capacity directly into
siege-breaking power, rewriting WAR-005's attrition calculus in the
besieger's favor — but only for states wealthy and organized enough to
sustain the cost, making artillery park size a first-rank, hard-to-fake
signal of centralized state power.
