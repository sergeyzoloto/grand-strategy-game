WWII commando units developing into permanent Cold War special-forces institutions — mechanically an extension of WAR-014's small-unit irregular doctrine and WAR-019's aviation-insertion capability rather than a genuinely new mechanism, but a real and enduring institutional form in its own right.

## Timing

British Commando units and the Special Operations Executive, and the American Office of Strategic Services, form from 1940 to conduct raiding, sabotage, and partisan-support operations behind enemy lines; several of these wartime organizations convert into permanent standing special-forces institutions (Britain's SAS, the CIA's paramilitary successor to the OSS) that remain active through the Cold War proxy conflicts (WAR-022) of the following decades.

## Mechanism

A small, intensively-trained unit inserted by air, sea, or infiltration behind enemy lines can conduct sabotage, intelligence-gathering, or partisan-support missions a conventional force could never reach or justify the cost of reaching — combining WAR-014's guerrilla-style small-unit tactics and reliance on irregular local support with WAR-019's aviation-insertion reach, applied by a conventional state's own forces rather than by an insurgent population.

## Social consequence

Converting wartime-improvised commando units into permanent peacetime institutions creates a small, elite, often semi-autonomous military career track outside conventional promotion and command structures, one whose operations frequently stay classified or officially deniable in a way conventional military action rarely is — an institutional form well-suited to the arm's-length, deniable operations WAR-022's proxy conflicts increasingly rely on.

## Regional specifics

Britain's and America's parallel wartime development of these units, followed by their institutionalization into standing Cold War-era special-forces organizations, established the template most other states' later special-operations forces explicitly modeled themselves on.

## Hook

A small, intensively-trained unit combining WAR-014's irregular small-unit tactics with WAR-019's aviation-insertion reach, institutionalized from a wartime improvisation into a permanent, often deniable standing capability well-suited to WAR-022's proxy-conflict era.
