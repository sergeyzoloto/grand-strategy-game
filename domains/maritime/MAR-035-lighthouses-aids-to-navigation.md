A fixed, maintained coastal marker — the shared public-good counterpart
to MAR-003's and MAR-009's onboard navigation technique, reducing wreck
risk for every vessel passing a hazard regardless of that vessel's own
crew's skill or local knowledge.

## Timing

The Pharos of Alexandria, completed around -280, is the archetypal
ancient case; systematic lighthouse-building as a maintained network
rather than isolated monuments develops in Europe from the medieval
period, with the Eddystone Rocks light (first built 1698, rebuilt in
durable stone by Smeaton by 1759) establishing open-ocean offshore
lighthouse construction as a solved engineering problem rather than a
sheltered-harbor-only technique.

## Mechanism

A fixed light or marker converts a specific navigational hazard from
something only a locally experienced crew can reliably avoid into
something any passing vessel can see and route around, regardless of
that crew's own knowledge — the investment only pays off where a hazard
is dangerous and well-trafficked enough to justify construction and
ongoing maintenance, usually funded through a toll on passing shipping
(as with England's Trinity House system) rather than being a universal
infrastructure every coastline needs equally.

## Social consequence

Because a lighthouse's benefit accrues to every vessel that passes,
regardless of whether that vessel's owner contributed to building or
maintaining it, funding one requires either a state willing to bear the
cost directly or an institution — a guild or chartered corporation like
Trinity House — with the authority to levy a toll on shipping that
benefits without being able to exclude non-payers from the light itself,
a public-goods funding problem distinct from any individual ship's own
technology or crew investment.

## Regional specifics

The Eddystone light's succession of failures — a wooden tower destroyed
by storm, a second destroyed by fire — before Smeaton's stone design
finally survives shows offshore lighthouse construction was a genuinely
hard engineering problem in its own right, not merely a matter of
placing a fire on a rock: interlocking dovetailed masonry and
underwater-setting cement were both necessary before an isolated offshore
light could reliably survive open-ocean conditions.

## Hook

A fixed, maintained coastal marker converts a navigational hazard into a
shared public good reducing wreck risk for every passing vessel
regardless of its own crew's skill — a civic infrastructure investment,
funded through tolls or state expenditure rather than any individual
ship's own technology, that sits alongside MAR-014's port infrastructure
as the domain's other major shore-based capital investment.
