# Thread: The Axial Age

Karl Jaspers's 1949 observation that several major civilizations
independently produce foundational ethical and philosophical breakthroughs
within a roughly overlapping, multi-century window (-800 to -200), with no
direct contact between most of the traditions involved. Whether this names a
real, causally-unified pattern or retrospectively imposes false unity on
independently-arising developments is a foundational, unresolved dispute —
model each linked entry as independently caused, clustering in time rather
than sharing a confirmed common driver.
