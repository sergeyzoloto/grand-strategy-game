# Thread: Literacy and Intermediaries

One variable — `intermediary dependence` — tracked across five thousand
years. Every entry below raises or lowers the cost of reaching the written
world without a specialist standing in between, and the arc does not run in
one direction: the gate falls, is rebuilt higher, falls again, is demolished,
is bypassed entirely, and is finally reconstituted in a form that denies being
a gate at all.

Spans MED, REL, PHI, LAW, COM, WAR, HLT and NAT. A links-only thread page per
`boundary-rules.md` — the mechanisms live in the entries, not here.

## The gate is built

- [[MED-010]] — The Emergence of Writing. A record makes a quantity checkable
  by someone absent, and arrives bundled with a scribal class.
- [[PHI-010]] — Pre-Axial Wisdom Literature. The scribal class instructing
  itself in how to occupy the position.
- [[LAW-020]] — Written Law and the Codification of Inequality. What the new
  medium does to law, and who controls the writing.
- [[FIN-020]] — Cadastre, Tax, and Census. The administrative apparatus the
  record makes possible.

## The gate falls

- [[MED-020]] — The Alphabet. Sign count collapses, and literacy can stop
  being a profession.

## The gate is rebuilt, higher

- [[REL-070]] — Canon. Doctrinal fixation as a power-allocating act.
- [[MED-040]] — Canon as the Fixation of a Language of Knowledge. The frozen
  language becomes a second gate, larger than the first.
- [[MED-060]] — Logographic Script and Written Unity. The opposite trade:
  reach across languages, bought with expensive readers.
- [[LAW-100]] — Meritocracy and State Bureaucracy. Literacy as the sole route
  to office, and a demand-side literacy engine running a millennium.
- [[REL-090]] — Religious Education. The institutions that train the gatekeepers.

## The gate falls again

- [[MED-070]] — Vernacular Translation. The only instrument that dissolves a
  gate made of language.
- [[MED-050]] — Papermaking. The substrate that changes the economics downstream.
- [[MED-080]] — The Printing Press. Reproduction cost collapses.
- [[REL-230]] — Printed Religion. The schism translation and print together
  produced, where a backed doctrinal quarrel already existed.
- [[HLT-190]] — Print, Silent Reading, and the Interior Self. What the new
  reading practice does to the reader.

## The gate is demolished

- [[MED-090]] — Newspapers and Periodical Journalism. Periodicity synchronises
  attention and constitutes the editor as a new intermediary.
- [[LAW-165]] — Compulsory Education. The training cost transferred to the
  state, which is what finally makes literacy general.
- [[WAR-200]] — Conscription and Mass Armies. The barracks as the largest adult
  education institution any nineteenth-century state operates.

## The gate is bypassed

- [[MED-100]] — The Telegraph. Information speed detaches from transport.
- [[MED-130]] — Broadcast Radio. One-to-many with no literacy requirement at
  all, reaching past editors, notables and clergy.
- [[MED-140]] — Television. Transmission stays cheap and content becomes
  expensive, so the medium that was cheap for the peripheral is not.

## The gate returns in a new form

- [[MED-150]] — The Global Network. The cost of being read falls to near zero,
  and the intermediary returns as the platform.
- [[LAW-225]] — Spectrum as Regulated Property. The legal object built to
  allocate access to a channel.
- [[PHI-410]] — Mass Culture and the Culture Industry. The critique of the
  arrangement, from inside it.

- [[MTH-020]] — The Algorithm as Delegable Procedure. The same move —

- [[COM-180]] — Technological Displacement of Craft. What happens to a class
