The infantry doctrine and standing-army financing gunpowder enables is
WAR-130's; this entry owns the chemistry and manufacturing technique
itself — refining saltpeter, formulating the charcoal-sulfur-saltpeter
mixture, and processing it into a usable, storable, reliably-combusting
product.

## Timing

Chinese alchemical texts describe gunpowder-type mixtures from around 850;
the formula and refined saltpeter-purification technique transmit through
the Middle East (Arabic and Persian alchemical documentation from around
1240) before reaching Europe, where the corning process — grinding the
mixture into uniform grains — matures through the 1400s.

## Mechanism

Gunpowder's explosive combustion depends on achieving a specific, carefully
balanced ratio of saltpeter, charcoal, and sulfur, with saltpeter's
purification being the hardest and most consequential step — impure
saltpeter burns unreliably and weakly. The later corning innovation
(processing the mixed powder into uniform granules rather than a loose
fine powder) solves a separate, subsequent problem: fine powder settles and
separates during transport and burns unevenly, while corned powder burns
more consistently and stores and transports far better, a manufacturing
refinement that matters as much to the weapon's practical usability as the
underlying chemistry itself.

## Social consequence

Gunpowder manufacture — saltpeter refining and mixture preparation
especially — requires specialized technical knowledge and, historically,
dedicated production facilities and supply chains distinct from ordinary
craft production, tying reliable gunpowder supply to the same standing
state fiscal and administrative capacity WAR-130 describes gunpowder
infantry itself requiring, one step further back in the supply chain.

## Regional specifics

The Middle Eastern alchemical tradition's detailed documentation of
saltpeter purification and gunpowder formulation makes it the crucial
transmission bridge between Chinese origination and eventual European
adoption, refining the technique along the way rather than merely relaying
it unchanged. European gunpowder development in this window is
substantially about manufacturing refinement (the corning process
specifically) rather than chemical origination — the underlying formula
and purification technique arrive already established via the Middle
Eastern route.

## Hook

Gunpowder's usability depends as much on manufacturing refinement (saltpeter
purification, then corning into uniform grains) as on the underlying
chemical formula — refinements that transmit and improve along a
China-to-Middle-East-to-Europe chain rather than originating independently
at each stop, directly gating the infantry doctrine WAR-130 covers on
whichever stage of this manufacturing chain a given region has reached.
