# Stats Observed

Append-only capture log of every mechanical variable invented while writing
entries. **Not a reconciled glossary** — no structure, no consistency enforcement,
no deduplication pass. If two entries independently invent near-synonyms, both
get logged as-is. Consolidation into an actual stat glossary happens once after
the full coverage pass, not while writing.

One line per invented variable, in the order encountered.

| stat name              | entry ID | definition                                                                 |
| ----------------------- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| legibility               | —        | state's ability to see/measure/tax a population or activity it governs.     |
| commensality             | —        | who eats with whom; a proxy for social boundary permeability.               |
| immunological capital    | —        | accumulated population-level disease resistance from prior exposure.        |
| toxic burden             | —        | cumulative harm from environmental/occupational exposure.                   |
| medical legitimacy       | —        | social authority granted to a medical practice, independent of its efficacy.|
| medical efficacy         | —        | actual health outcome improvement a medical practice produces.              |
| antibiotic pool          | —        | depleting stock of effective antimicrobial treatment, drawn down by use and resistance. |
| information speed        | —        | rate at which news/knowledge propagates across distance.                    |
| protection rent          | —        | value extracted by an actor in exchange for (real or imposed) protection.   |
| route knowledge          | —        | accumulated, learnable knowledge of a trade or travel route.                |
| time discipline          | —        | internalized scheduling of labor/life to clock or calendar rather than task.|
| privacy                  | —        | degree to which household/individual activity is shielded from communal observation. |
| contract enforcement     | —        | reliability with which an agreement is upheld absent personal trust.        |
| bullion stock            | COM-011  | accumulated specie held by a polity or market, independent of its fiscal use. |
| price level              | COM-011  | aggregate price index for a market/region, movable by monetary and non-monetary shocks. |
| sovereign credit capacity | COM-011 | a state's ability to borrow against future revenue without defaulting.      |
| migration range          | MIG-001  | sustainable travel distance for a population or raiding party, per mobility technology available. |
| settler footprint        | MIG-004  | founding/settling population size a colonization event requires relative to its resulting control. |
| displacement pressure    | MIG-006  | accumulated forced-movement stress propagating through a population or contact chain. |
| confederation cohesion   | MIG-007  | how strongly a loose multi-group political-military alliance holds together, tied to ongoing success. |
| diaspora reach           | MIG-009  | size/connectivity of an established diaspora's trust and trade network abroad. |
| migration trigger intensity | MIG-014 | how sharply a discovery or opportunity spikes migration above steady-state levels. |
| migration cost           | MIG-015  | price/risk/time a migrant must pay to relocate, before any legal barrier is applied. |
| urban absorption capacity | MIG-016 | a city's ability to house/employ/service incoming migrants without strain. |
| border friction          | MIG-017  | legal/administrative barrier to movement imposed independent of migration cost. |
| skill differential       | MIG-019  | gap in institutional/career opportunity driving skilled-labor migration.   |
| naval prestige           | MAR-018  | a state's naval standing as measured by internationally-comparable capital-ship metrics, resettable to near-zero by a single capital-ship design leap. |
| religious authority      | REL-001  | trust/legitimacy a religious institution commands, usable as a resource for banking-like trust, tithe/court authority, and canon-enforcement — distinct from medical or state legitimacy. |
| intellectual authority   | PHI-001  | prestige/trust a school of thought or reasoning tradition commands, tracking sustained patronage and disciple-training capacity rather than a founder's original argument alone. |
| habitus formation        | PHI-004  | degree to which repeated ritual/practice reliably reshapes a performer's internalized disposition — a stat implementation of design principle 2, reused across ritual, ascetic, and meditative practice traditions. |
