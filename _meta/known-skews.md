# Known Skews

Structural imbalances in the corpus that are **known and accepted**, not defects
awaiting a fix. Recorded here so a future audit doesn't rediscover the same
pattern and propose padding as the remedy — see design principle 5 (asymmetry is
modelled as difference, not penalty) and the standing rule against padding for
symmetry's own sake.

## 1850–1950 density concentration

Five domains independently densify their own entry count in the same
century, for the same underlying reason: this is where the best-documented
history concentrates, for genuinely contingent reasons (industrialization's
actual geographic center of gravity), not domain-specific bias.

- **Health** — antisepsis through the therapeutic turn
- **Engineering** — the machine-tool/steel/semiconductor chain
- **War** — the mechanized-warfare sequence
- **Finance** — the gold-standard-through-Depression run
- **Nature** — the 1661–1935 classical-physics sequence (the sharpest single
  regional-balance number in the corpus, 42.9% Europe+Mediterranean, correctly
  traced by Nature's own audit to this one topic cluster rather than systemic
  bias — every individual entry in it credits non-European contributions
  honestly where they exist)

No single domain's own regional-balance check was positioned to catch this,
because "is my domain's regional balance acceptable" and "does the whole
project's density spike in the same hundred years across five domains at
once" are different questions — each domain audited its own slice correctly
and moved on. Originally identified in the cross-domain synthesis,
`_meta/audits/00-cross-domain-2026-09-08.md` §6.

**For scenario design:** a player moving through this wiki in date order will
experience a five-domain-wide acceleration and Europe/Americas concentration
across exactly one century. Model this as a real feature of the underlying
history being represented, not an artifact to smooth over. Do **not** resolve
it by adding non-European entries to these domains for symmetry — that
would be padding, which the project's standing rules reject regardless of
what it's padding toward.
